# Authored-content and real-use quality

After every important learner-visible behavior or content change, follow the complete
[authoring review sequence](../../../../content/AUTHORING.md#authoring-loop) and
[semantic acceptance rubric](../../../../content/AUTHORING.md#semantic-quality-rubric). Inspect the
whole Expedition document and every cited source, including content not reached during real use.

For Critical Thinking, complete the journey: auth/profile, adoption/activation, Lesson and every
required Activity, wrong-to-correct paths for all families, known/clear/restoration, Support
open/refresh/hide/restore and referenced grading, every Leg Guardian and the Expedition Guardian,
recovery/retreat/resume/rematch/first-win rewards, leaderboard, refresh/sign-in persistence, and a
second learner's isolation.

For each other Expedition, complete at least one Leg through its Guardian and exercise every family,
one Support Path, and representative calibration/reward behavior. These are review requirements;
the [real-backend web rig](../../../../apps/learner-app/e2e-realuse/README.md) owns its narrower
implemented UI coverage claims.
