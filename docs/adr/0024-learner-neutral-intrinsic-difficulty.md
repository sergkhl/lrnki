# Author learner-neutral comparative difficulty bands

Status: Accepted

Each Stop's authored difficulty band compares reasoning and coordination demanded within its
Expedition. Difficulty informs presentation and pacing; it neither establishes mastery nor creates
prerequisites. Authored prerequisites remain the route constraint.

Graph position and source density do not measure cognitive demand, and bands do not claim an
absolute scale across domains. Population-level adaptation remains deferred until graded evidence
and real-use evaluation justify it.
