# Author learner-neutral comparative difficulty bands

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Each authored Stop carries a learner-neutral comparative difficulty band. The author judges the
reasoning and coordination demanded within that Expedition; prerequisites remain the actual route
constraint and are not inferred from difficulty.

Difficulty is presentation and pacing input, not learner mastery, a hidden prerequisite, or a claim
that one absolute scale transfers across domains. Population-level adaptation remains deferred until
real graded evidence and real-use evaluation justify it.

## Context

The game benefits from a visible challenge curve, but deriving difficulty from graph position or
source density confused architecture with cognitive demand. Direct bands preserve the useful prior
without inventing a second route.
