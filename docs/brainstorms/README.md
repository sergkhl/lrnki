# Brainstorms

Brainstorms are temporary working documents for shaping a possible change before it becomes a plan,
ADR, or live TODO item.

Once the work is accepted for implementation, link the brainstorm from a ready/in-progress plan rather
than copying its requirements. Once the work is completed or abandoned, fold durable decisions into
ADRs, terminology into `CONTEXT.md`, and current status or completed outcome summaries into
`docs/plans/TODO.md`; then delete the brainstorm. Do not keep completed brainstorms here as an archive;
git history is the provenance record.
