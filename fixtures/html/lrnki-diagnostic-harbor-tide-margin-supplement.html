North Quay Tide Margin Supplement

This supplement defines the tide-margin input used by the Harbor Dispatch Core Protocol. It applies to a particular vessel, planned channel, and planned transit time.

Tide margin

Tide margin is the predicted minimum water depth along the planned channel at the planned transit time minus the vessel's required water depth in its current loading condition. The calculation uses the lowest predicted channel depth, not the harbor's average depth.

Tide height and tide margin are different quantities. Tide height describes water level relative to a chart reference. Tide margin additionally depends on channel depth and the required water depth of the particular vessel. Two vessels at the same tide height can therefore have different tide margins.

Adequate margin

A North Quay ferry has adequate tide margin when its calculated tide margin is at least 0.8 metres. A positive margin below 0.8 metres is not adequate for this protocol. The threshold applies to North Quay ferry departures; it is not a universal threshold for every harbor or vessel class.

Adequate tide margin is necessary for ordinary release, but it is not movement authority. Harbor Control must still create a berth reservation, validate the dispatch token during the clearance window, and confirm that no safety override is active.

Forecast updates

A forecast revision is the identified issue of the channel-depth forecast used to calculate tide margin for one planned transit time.

The calculation is tied to the forecast revision used for the planned transit time. If a newer forecast lowers the predicted minimum channel depth before movement is authorized, Harbor Control recalculates the margin. An earlier adequate result does not remain valid merely because a dispatch token has already been issued.

Movement authorization

Movement authorization is Harbor Control's explicit permission for one named vessel to enter and move through the planned channel. Adequate tide margin alone is not movement authorization. Harbor Control grants it only after the reservation, dispatch token, clearance window, and safety-state checks required by the core protocol succeed.

Example

At 10:20, the predicted minimum channel depth is 4.6 metres. Ferry Cedar requires 3.7 metres in its current loading condition, so its tide margin is 0.9 metres and is adequate. Ferry Dune requires 4.0 metres under the same forecast, so its 0.6-metre margin is positive but not adequate for North Quay release.
