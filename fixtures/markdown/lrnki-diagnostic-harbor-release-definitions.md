# North Quay Release Definitions Addendum

This addendum supplies operational definitions used when the Harbor Dispatch Core Protocol and the
North Quay Tide Margin Supplement are read together. It does not replace either source; all three
registered documents describe the same North Quay departure procedure.

North Quay staff use the operator handbook to consult these definitions. The handbook records the
procedure; it does not grant Harbor Control authority, authorize channel entry, change the
reservation registry, issue a channel-depth forecast, determine a loading condition, or set a
vessel's required water depth.

## Channel-depth forecast

A **channel-depth forecast** is a time-stamped prediction of the minimum water depth along one named
channel for a specified transit time. It supplies the predicted depth used to calculate tide margin;
it does not grant movement permission.

## Harbor Control authority

**Harbor Control authority** is the operational power assigned to Harbor Control to create or
suspend berth reservations and to authorize or halt movement through North Quay's channel. A token,
timetable entry, or operator handbook does not carry that authority.

## Reservation registry

The **reservation registry** is Harbor Control's current official record of berth reservations. For
each reservation identifier, it records the named vessel, named berth, active or suspended status,
and applicable clearance window against which a dispatch token is validated.

## Release sequence

The **release sequence** is the ordered procedure in which Harbor Control first confirms adequate
tide margin, then creates an active berth reservation, then issues its dispatch token, then validates
that token during the clearance window, and finally authorizes movement only when no safety override
is active.

## Channel entry authorization

**Channel entry authorization** is Harbor Control's permission for one named vessel to cross from
outside into the planned channel during its active clearance window. It permits the entry crossing,
but a safety override may still halt movement afterward.

## Loading condition

A vessel's **loading condition** is the vessel-specific amount and distribution of everything carried
aboard at the planned transit time. The loading condition is current input to that vessel's required
water depth; one vessel's loading condition cannot be substituted for another's.

## Vessel required water depth

**Vessel required water depth** is the minimum water depth that one named vessel needs to use the
planned channel safely at the planned transit time under its current loading condition. This is the
vessel-specific quantity subtracted from predicted minimum channel depth when tide margin is
calculated.

During an ordinary departure, the current forecast and loading condition establish the two depth
inputs. Harbor Control then follows the release sequence using its registry and authority. Neither
the source documents nor the operator handbook performs any of those operational actions.
