ISLANDED MICROGRID RESERVE PROCEDURE

Purpose

The fictional Rowan Microgrid can operate in grid-connected mode or islanded mode. Grid-connected
mode allows the external grid to balance short changes in demand. Islanded mode disconnects the
microgrid from the external grid, so local storage and generation must balance demand.

Reserve floor

The reserve floor is a control target used only while the Rowan Microgrid is islanded. It requires
the battery state-of-charge estimate to remain at or above 30 percent during ordinary islanded
operation. The reserve floor is not a universal minimum for grid-connected operation, battery
shipping, maintenance, or emergency shutdown.

Crossing below 30 percent does not mean the battery contains no energy. It means ordinary islanded
dispatch has crossed the configured reserve target. The safety controller may disconnect loads or
shut down the battery at a different limit.

State-of-charge estimate

The state-of-charge estimate is the controller's current estimate of usable battery energy expressed
as a percentage of the configured usable capacity. It is a measurement-derived representation, not
the stored energy itself. Copying the estimate to a dashboard does not copy energy, alter the battery,
or guarantee that the estimate remains current.

The controller marks an estimate stale when no accepted sensor update has arrived for sixty seconds.
A stale estimate must not authorize an ordinary discharge command, even when its displayed value is
above the reserve floor.

Ordinary islanded discharge

An ordinary islanded discharge command is eligible only when all of the following are true:

1. The microgrid is in islanded mode.
2. The state-of-charge estimate is current.
3. The estimated state of charge is above the 30 percent reserve floor.
4. No battery safety interlock is active.

These conditions make a command eligible; they do not require the controller to discharge. The
dispatch controller may continue charging or hold the current output when local generation already
meets demand.

Emergency override

During a declared emergency, the emergency controller may authorize discharge below the ordinary
reserve floor to supply the protected medical circuit. This exception does not redefine the reserve
floor and does not make below-floor discharge ordinary operation. The battery safety interlock still
has priority and may refuse the emergency command.

Recovery

After grid service returns, the microgrid first synchronizes with the external grid, then closes the
grid connection, and only then leaves islanded mode. The islanded reserve floor stops governing
ordinary dispatch after the mode transition completes. A grid-available signal by itself does not
complete that transition.
